# MkCuts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MkCuts ("the app", "we"), a desktop application for content creators, handles information. MkCuts is designed to keep your data on your own computer.

## 1. Where your data is stored

- **Your projects stay on your computer.** Videos, scripts, captions, narration and project files are saved in a folder on your own PC. We do not upload them to our servers.
- **Connected accounts.** When you connect TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, the access tokens issued by those platforms are stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's protection (Windows DPAPI). We do not receive or store them on any server.

## 2. Information we access from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, MkCuts requests only:

- **user.info.basic** — your display name, avatar and account ID, to show which account is connected;
- **video.publish** and **video.upload** — to post a video you chose to your account, or send it to your TikTok drafts.

Before posting, the app also reads your account's posting options (such as allowed privacy levels and whether comments, duets or stitches are enabled) so it can show you the correct choices. MkCuts does not read your followers, messages, likes or other private data, and does not post anything without your action.

## 3. When data leaves your computer

Data is sent only to services needed for a feature you use:

- **Publishing platforms (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook):** the video file, caption and the posting options you selected, when you click publish or send to drafts.
- **AI and voice providers you configure** (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft Azure, ElevenLabs): the text needed to write scripts, suggest titles or generate narration, using your own API keys and subject to those providers' privacy policies.
- **Stock media sources** (Wikimedia Commons, Pixabay, Pexels): search terms used to find images and videos for your scenes.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers, and we do not use it to train AI models.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

- Everything MkCuts stores is on your computer. You can delete your projects at any time from the app or from the output folder, and uninstalling the app removes it.
- You can disconnect any account in the app (Accounts → Disconnect), which deletes its stored tokens. You can also revoke MkCuts' access in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions).

## 5. Children

MkCuts is not intended for children under the minimum age required by the platforms it connects to.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your location (including under Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data. Since your data is stored on your own device, you control it directly; for any request or question, contact us below.
